# Preferred canonical strategy/tip/lesson names

Use these EXACT strings (verbatim) whenever a book's content matches one of these concepts, even if the book's wording differs. This is critical so results merge cleanly across many parallel reviewers. If a concept doesn't fit any of these, invent a new short canonical name (Title Case, 3-8 words, e.g. "Diversify Across Uncorrelated Assets") and use that consistently.

## Risk management
- Cut Losses Short, Let Winners Run
- Always Use a Stop-Loss
- Never Risk More Than 1-2% of Capital Per Trade
- Position Sizing Based on Risk
- Diversify Across Uncorrelated Assets
- Avoid Overleveraging / Excessive Margin
- Risk-Reward Ratio Before Entering a Trade
- Don't Average Down on a Losing Position
- Preserve Capital First, Profit Second
- Have a Predefined Exit Plan Before Entering

## Trading psychology / discipline
- Control Emotions: Avoid Fear and Greed
- Follow Your Trading Plan/System Without Deviation
- Avoid Revenge Trading After a Loss
- Patience: Wait for High-Probability Setups
- Keep a Trading Journal
- Avoid Overtrading
- Accept Losses as a Cost of Doing Business
- Discipline Beats Intelligence in Trading
- Don't Let Ego Affect Trading Decisions
- Manage Expectations: Trading Is Not Gambling

## Technical analysis
- Follow the Trend / Trade With the Trend
- Use Moving Averages to Identify Trend
- Support and Resistance Levels
- Volume Confirms Price Action
- Chart Patterns (Head and Shoulders, Triangles, Flags)
- Candlestick Patterns Signal Reversals/Continuations
- Use Multiple Timeframes for Confirmation
- Momentum Indicators (RSI, MACD, Stochastics)
- Breakout Trading Above Resistance/Below Support
- Avoid Trading Against the Prevailing Trend
- Fibonacci Retracement Levels

## Fundamental analysis / investing
- Buy Low, Sell High (Value Investing)
- Invest in Companies You Understand
- Margin of Safety When Valuing a Stock
- Analyze Financial Statements Before Investing
- Long-Term Investing Beats Market Timing
- Dollar-Cost Averaging
- Earnings Growth Drives Stock Price
- Assess Management Quality and Competitive Advantage (Moat)
- Diversify Across Asset Classes
- Compound Interest / Reinvest Returns

## Money management / personal finance
- Live Below Your Means / Save Consistently
- Build an Emergency Fund
- Avoid High-Interest Consumer Debt
- Pay Yourself First
- Understand and Manage Credit Scores
- Set Clear Financial Goals

## Market behavior / macro
- Markets Are Cyclical (Bull and Bear Cycles)
- Interest Rates Affect Asset Prices
- Supply and Demand Drive Price
- Contrarian Investing: Be Fearful When Others Are Greedy
- Don't Try to Time the Market Perfectly
- Liquidity Matters When Entering/Exiting Positions

## Options / derivatives / forex specific
- Understand Implied Volatility Before Trading Options
- Hedge Positions With Options/Futures
- Time Decay (Theta) Erodes Option Value
- Currency Pairs Move on Interest Rate Differentials and News

## Process / systems
- Backtest a Trading System Before Using Real Money
- Keep Trading Systems Simple
- Adapt Strategy to Market Conditions
- Continuous Education and Self-Improvement
- Plan the Trade, Trade the Plan
