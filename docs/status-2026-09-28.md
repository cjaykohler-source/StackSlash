## What's research-only (not shipped, not a trigger)

The **breakout-event study** — full detail in `docs/breakout-study.md`.
Short version:

- Defined and validated every >=75% single-session gain in the $0.10-$5
  band since 2016: **1,465 events, 974 symbols**, after discovering and
  guarding against a real class of data artifacts (reverse-split/reorg
  equity cancellations that a naive filter — and the project's own
  `corporate_actions` table — both miss).
- Built two reusable research scripts and a wide, persisted per-event
  feature dataset (67k rows) so future questions don't each re-derive
  their own slice of the warehouse.
- The headline finding: a quiet-volume streak paired with a trailing
  20-day price decline of >=20% shows a real, both-periods-independent
  lift (up to 3.25x baseline in 2016-21, 2.61x in 2022+) — but the
  absolute hit rate is still low (best cell ~1.6%) and this has **not**
  been checked against a permutation-based noise floor yet. Not a
  trigger candidate as-is.
- A broader 15-metric scan mostly found one collinear archetype
  ("beaten-down, cheap, illiquid, volatile") rather than several
  independent patterns — flagged explicitly rather than overclaimed.
- A full data audit for expanding this: two paid-for, already-loaded,
  still-unused resources (`edgar_facts.parquet` for dilution, the 85GB
  minute warehouse for intraday microstructure) are the next step before
  reaching for anything requiring new paid access (historical short
  interest, borrow-fee data, full order-book/L2 data — all real budget
  decisions, not research tasks).

Also from this session: two trigger-scorecard audit findings
(`avoid_chase_extended`'s live volume gate vs. its backtest — resolved,
not a bug; `avoid_reverse_split`'s window vs. its backtest — still open,
no code change made).
